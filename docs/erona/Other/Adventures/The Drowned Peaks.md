#### Overview
Sovereign [[Andros]] has put out a call for an expedition to find the lost capital of [[Mycea]]. Various factions have put forward their champions to assist in the endeavour, each with their own values and goals. 

The [[Sect of Illumination]] has tasked its [[Qedhi]] craftsmen with the construction of the *Sunglass*, a device powered by [[Phasonomy]] which can pierce the sea to find the lost capital even from above the waves. First, however, the rough location of the drowned city needs to be found in the lost archives of the abandoned Sanctums located on the [[Barrier Islands]]. 

Alternatively, all players choose one faction on whose behalf they're attempting to find Mycea. The other factions will have their own expeditions. 


#### Factions
##### The [[Cenerian League]]
The League is sending one of its top Sea Lords to advance its interests. They are particularly curious to find any evidence on Andros' claim of lineage linking him directly to the last Sovereign Alvos. 
Recommended class: Fighter

##### The [[Seventh Sanctum]]
The Sanctum has a selfish interest in finding the lost archives of Mycea to expand their own collection. They are also wary of letting the [[Sect of Pyos]] get to these lost texts first.
Recommended class: Wizard / Bard (Lore)

##### The [[Sect of Illumination]]
The Eluminoi are main backers of this excursion, with one of their priests chosen to lead it by Andros himself. Mycea is a holy site to the sect, and would reinvigorate the people's trust in both them and Andros should it be found. 
Recommended class: Cleric (Light) / Warlock (Celestial)

##### The [[Sect of Pyos]]
The Pyosians are mistrustful of the operation, but would not pass up the opportunity that it could represent, should the fabled city be found. They are particularly anxious about proving that their creed was part of early [[Mycean]] culture. 
Recommended class: Cleric (Order) / Paladin

##### The [[Sigodo]]
Known for their cunning and close-quarter expertise, the Sigodo would be an ideal candidate faction to hire for a cunning rogue to delve into the deeper areas of the archives and lost city. Whether they can be trusted remains unsure. 
Recommended class: Rogue / Shadow Monk

##### Other Factions
Smaller factions would also be interested in sending their own. Trading Fellowships from [[Cenerian]] cities would be keen to send their envoys. [[Thalen]] nobles would join the expedition out of adventurous spirit. [[Dunwarden]] rangers might make excellent guides, even in new terrain. [[Anchorites]] monks could be seen as impartial by the other factions.
