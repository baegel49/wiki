Except for rare cases, every character has an Echo. [[Beings/Echoes|Echoes]] are (mostly) permanent aspects of one's being, and are used to channel (one specific type of) magic. You can bind up to INT / 20 Echoes in your mind. For all intents and purposes, an Echo is synonymous with a spell.
The only way to learn more spells is to capture another's Echo and bind it in your mind, on paper/parchment, objects, or even the earth, using Arcanum, Phasonomy, or Geomancery, respectively. 

When creating your Echo, consult the table below for ideas as to its possible effects. Echoes are shaped by the likes, wants, and desires of their hosts, and your Echo will reflect your life experience. Echoes can change over time, but are often immutable by the time your character becomes an adult, unless undergoing severe life changes or trauma. 

SD = Amount of spell dice used, SUM = the sum of the spell dice rolled.

Echoes are not limitless in power. Roughly speaking, a character has 5 points to spend on their Echo's abilities:

### Channelling your Echo
Echoes are channelled through your Presence stat. Divide your Presence by 20 (rounding down). This is how many spell dice (SD) you have available to you innately, a measure of your echo's ability. 

In all cases, how many SD you commit to the spell is how effective it is. It also has a higher chance of going wrong. 
Rolling **doubles** on your SD results in a falter roll being made.
Rolling **triples** requires an Ego save. On a success, trigger a falter roll. On a failure, suffer a Mind Wound.
Rolling **quadruples** or higher results in a Mind Wound and a falter roll.

| 1D10 | Mind Wound                                                                                                                                     |
| ---- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 0    | **Flinch.** You lose your next action.                                                                                                         |
| 1    | **Headache.** [-] on your next roll. Suffer 1 TOLL.                                                                                            |
| 2    | **Nosebleed.** Take 1d5 damage. [-] on Presence checks until you rest.                                                                         |
| 3    | **Ringing.** Your ears ring and your vision swims. [-] on all rolls for 1d10 rounds.                                                           |
| 4    | **Aphasia.** Your words come out wrong or not at all. You can’t speak coherently or cast for 1d10 minutes.                                     |
| 5    | **Memory Gap.** You lose the last 1d10 hours. You also forget one Trained skill until your next full rest.                                     |
| 6    | **Black Out.** You collapse unconcious for this round and the next. Lose the ability to cast the spell for until you rest.                     |
| 7    | **Seizure.** You fall prone and convulse for 1d5 rounds, taking 1d10 damage. [-] on Presence checks and Ego saves until treated.               |
| 8    | **Hemorrhage.** Blood from the eyes and ears. You’re unconscious for 1d10 hours and permanently lose 1d10 Presence, which also lowers your SD. |
| 9    | **Coma.** The Echo escapes and you lose the spell permanently. You are unconscious until roused by extraordinary means.                        |
