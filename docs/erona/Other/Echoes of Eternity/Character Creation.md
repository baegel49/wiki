### Stats

##### Strength
Measure of your raw power. Divide by 10 for unarmed damage and how many inventory slots you have.
##### Speed
How fast you are under pressure. Divide by 20 for your innate Armour Points (AP).
##### Intellect
How capable you are with your mind. Divide by 20 for how many Echoes you can bind mentally. 
##### Presence
Your will and charisma manifest. Divide by 20 for how powerful your Echo is and gain that many Spell Dice (SD). 
##### Combat
Your ability to fight. Divide by 10 for impact bonus dealt by weapons. 

### Saves
##### Ego
Your ability to resist being swayed, succumbing to madness, resisting enchantment, and holding on to your sense of self. 
##### Fear
Your ability to resist your primal instinct of flight, how you react against frightening monsters and situations. 
##### Body
Your ability to get out of harm's way, resist hunger and disease. 
##### Faith
Your ability to delude yourself into thinking all of this is part of the gods' plan. Faith can be substituted for nearly any other save (use common sense) as your Echo attempts to save you by sheer power of belief in your god. Faith is lowered by 1 whenever you fail (including suffering 1 toll), and by 1d5 every time you critically fail (including a panic check). It can be raised by 1 whenever you critically succeed (instead of relieving 1 toll). 

### Class
##### Warrior
A capable fighter. 
+25 Combat
+20 Body Save
+15 Fear Save
+1 Max Wounds
Skills: Martial Training, Reckoning. 2 Trained Skills OR 1 Expert Skill.
##### Sage
A master within their specialty. 
+20 Intellect
+25 Ego Save
+10 Presence
+1 Spell Dice
Skills: 1 Master Skill + prerequisites. 1 Trained Skill.
##### Cleric
A servant of the faith. 
+50 Faith Save
+20 Presence
-10 Ego Save
+1 Max Wounds
Skills: Rites. 2 Trained Skills OR 1 Expert Skill.
##### Adept
A cunning expert. 
+10 to all Stats except one.
+15 to all Saves except one. 
+1 Luck
Skills: Athletics. 2 Trained Skills AND 1 Expert Skill.
