##### Fast
Fast weapons can attack twice in one round.
##### Mounted
Can only be used while mounted.
##### Parry
A weapon with parry acts as an AP(X) shield if the wielder has no shield equipped.
##### Reach
A weapon with reach has advantage on combat checks in the first round against non-reach weapons.
##### Skill
If the PC has the relevant skill learned, add double the usual combat modifier to the weapon's impact. 
##### Sundering 
Degrades armour and shields twice as fast.
##### Topple
Weapons with topple have [+] on knocking enemies prone, foregoing all damage.
##### Two-Handed
Requires the use of both hands at all times.
##### Unwieldy
Unwieldy weapons always go last, even with a successful combat check.
