Except for rare cases, every character has an Echo. Echoes are (mostly) permanent aspects of one's being, and are used to channel (one specific type of) magic. You can bind up to (INT/20) Echoes in your mind.
The only way to learn more spells is to capture another's Echo and bind it in paper, objects, or even the earth, using Arcanum, Phasonomy, or Geomancery, respectively. 

| D10 | Nature of Echo    |
| --- | ----------------- |
| 1   | Null              |
| 2   | Transform         |
| 3   | Slow/Hasten       |
| 4   | Weaken/Strengthen |
| 5   | Protect           |
| 6   | Damage            |
| 7   | Restrain/Unleash  |
| 8   | Enchant/Compel    |
| 9   | Conjure           |
| 10  | Utility           |
These are just guidelines. 
For instance, Karn the Sage has a history of fire, being born near a burning orphanage. This has imprinted on his echo, and he channels it to damage those he deems deserving. He calls this spell "Fireball", and it damages everything in an SDx5' area via fiery explosion for the SUM of SD rolled.
Kara is less violently inclined, and her echo is capable of conjuring illusions. She can conjure an SDx5' illusion for as long as she concentrates on it. Alternatively, she can conjure SD amount of illusions roughly her own size. 
Her twin sister Lara has the opposite gift, that of invisibility. She can cast invisibility on a creature or object for SDx10 minutes, but the spell weakens with movement. Each round of movement reduces the duration by 10 minutes.

### Channelling your Echo
Echoes are channelled through your Presence stat. Divide your Presence by 20 (rounding down). This is how many spell dice (SD) you have available to you innately, a measure of your echo's ability. 

In all cases, how many SD you commit to the spell is how effective it is. It also has a higher chance of going wrong. Rolling doubles on your SD results in a mishap, and an Ego save needs to be made. If this is as part of your own Echo, you simply suffer TOLL on a failure. If the spell was cast through an Echo other than your own, more dangerous things can occur: 

| 1D6 | Mishap                                                                                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | The Echo escapes. Lose the spell permanently.                                         |
| 2   | Lose the ability to cast the spell for a day.                                         |
| 3   | The spell has the opposite effect, targeting you if harmful or enemies if beneficial. |
| 4   | The spell fizzles violently. Take 1d10+5 damage and suffer 1d5 TOLL.                  |
| 5   | The spell fizzles chaotically. Suffer 1d5 TOLL.                                       |
| 6   | The spell fizzles harmlessly. Recover 1 TOLL.                                         |
