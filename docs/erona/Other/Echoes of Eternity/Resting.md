### Short Rests
1-2 hours of catching your breath. 
Make a REST SAVE by using your worst save in unsafe areas and your best in safe areas. 
If you succeed, reduce your Toll by one. A critical fail results in taking toll.
Gain 1d5 Resistance.
**Flesh Wounds** are healed during Short Rests.
**Minor Injuries** can be attempted to be healed using the appropriate tools, skills, or decoctions.
Roll all expended SD dice. Results of 1-4 are returned to your pool. 
**Spells** can be moved from scrolls or books and committed to the mind, or vice versa. 

Short rests are modified by:

| Item             | Effect |
| ---------------- | :----: |
| No Food          |  -25   |
| Rations          |  -10   |
| No Shelter       |  [-]   |
| Fine Camping Kit |  +10   |
| Cooked Food      |  +10   |

### Long Rests
A couple days of rest. 
Convert half your TOLL into stats / saves.
Resistance is restored fully.
All injuries can be attempted to be healed using the appropriate tools, skills, or decoctions. 
Disease can be healed using the appropriate medicine, decoctions, or seeing a Physicker.
