The Sect of the Lodestar is a quasi-religious group of astrologers who study the night sky and its movements to interpret future events on [[Erona]]. They are especially worried about a new [[Collapse]] caused by another falling star. Various astrologers across the world have their own beliefs as to what the stars actually are, and the Sect is completely decentralised. Many astrologers exist without being part of the sect, though any who study the night sky are welcome in its ranks. 

The Sect of the Lodestar is a relatively new sect, being fused from two older institutions, one Cenerian and one Thalen. The Cenerian one traces its origins to [[Mycean]] days, when the astrologers used the "ship's star" to navigate. Current astrologers are at odds as to whether this was the same star they call the Lodestar or whether it was a separate star which has now been forgotten, or has disappeared. The second, Thalen group, were skilled navigators who used the Lodestar to navigate the [[Karrow]] during the [[Thalen Migration]] and who have introduced the word "Lodestar" to Ceneria, who previously and still are referring to it as the "still" or "steadfast star".
