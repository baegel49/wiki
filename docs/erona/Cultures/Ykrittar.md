The Ykritta are a secluded and isolated people of the north. They have little contact with the rest of [[Osera]] but are relatively well-known due to their regular appearance as boogeymen in children's tales. 

> "If you don't eat your soup, an Ykrittar will come and take you away to the far icy wastes of the North and throw you in their stew!"

The Ykritta are known for their hardiness, and have recently begun trading with the [[Erona]]n south, resulting in advancement of their shipbuilding based on [[Cenerian]] designs. In turn, this has allowed for the Ykritta to become expert whalers in the [[Lotan Sea]], trading blubber, meat, and whale oil in [[Enetia]].
