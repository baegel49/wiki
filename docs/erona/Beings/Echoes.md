Echoes are minor spirits believed to exist in all living things, though are particularly strong in specific people under certain conditions, especially if they are aware of the Echo's existence. They are considered mirrors of the soul, though can have their own personality quirks. They are referred to as 'Daemons' by scholars of the [[Seventh Sanctum]], 'Fravash' by [[Rurii]] shamans, 'Nim(s)' by [[Brethiln]] druids. The [[Sect of Illumination]] and [[Sect of Pyos]] consider them superstitious and even heretical, believing that magic only comes from the gods. 

It is believed that Echoes can be animated to perform magic through various ways of interaction, including (but not limited to): [[Arcanum]], [[Phasonomy]], [[Geomancery]], meditation, hallucinogenic episodes (substance or experience-induced), or, in rare cases, by simply conversing with oneself (if the Echo is receptive/strong enough). 

Most people are not aware of their Echo, and when channelled (for instance under high duress), often provoke chaotic outcomes. Those who are not in touch with their Echo can induce these effects through consumption of [[Haze]]. 

It is believed by certain scholars that [[Lethe]] have no Echoes, and are instead made from the same substance as Echoes themselves, whatever that might be.
