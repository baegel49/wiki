God (with human origin) of oaths, laws, and civilisation, represented by an eye. Worshipped by the [[Sect of Pyos]]. Originally worshipped by [[Mycean]]s, then [[Cenerian]]s, and now popular among the [[Thalen]], who initially called him **Tioric** but have begun adopting the Cenerian word (though often pronounce it as Pjoss). 

Pyos is believed to have founded the ancient city of [[Mycea]] and ensuring its dominance by giving its people the knowledge of [[Arcanum]]. The [[Seventh Sanctum]] believes worship of the god is much younger, however.

Pyos is actually a fairly new deity, adapted from the Akkurim god known as Ennu, whose symbol was also an Eye, and was the god of order and civilisation. Pyos was worshipped after the Collapse by Akkurim supporters after the war, their need for secrecy eventually resulting in the knowledge of Ennu dying out. 

The Sect of Pyos does not know this per se (maybe only the upper echelons) but are fighting hard to find long lost scrolls and tomes that could confirm the god's existence since Mycean days.
