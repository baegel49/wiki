Thalendor is the realm located in central [[Osera]], with its capital at [[Ostellyth]]. For the first time in their history, the [[Thalen]] people are united. After securing both the [[Thalen Migration|migration]] and victory over the [[Brethiln]], there was little doubt that [[Aldric]] would unite his people under one banner, and so he did. What was left out of that story was that the bureaucratic might of the [[Sect of Pyos]] allowed [[Aldric]] to codify his people's laws and institutionally rule them. Now, the [[Thalen]] are the dominant culture in central [[Osera]], and [[Aldric]]'s son [[Normund]] rules with an iron fist.

The kingdom is divided into great holdings, ruled by Hertogs:

Central lands:

- Ecardun - ruled by Ecard II
- Crownlands - ruled directly by [[Normund]]
- Highkeep - ruled by Merovin
- Falcgard - ruled by Falcor II
- Merryn
- Donadun

Northern lands (Northern Marches): 
- Northwatch - ruled by Balghin
- Dinven - ruled by Bathert

Eastern lands (Eastern Marches): 

- Osmark - ruled by Siggort
- Lanvar - ruled by Brennock

Southern lands (Eindellyon): 

- Corryn - ruled by Theodmar
- Lythmark - ruled by Norvan
- Olervan - ruled by Sigholt

The Great Holdings are further divided into Manorlands, smaller parcels each overseen by a Manorlord, or Groff. 

The various castles that dot the Thalen landscape directly belong to the king, however. Each castellan is appointed by and loyal to the crown, a fact which the Hertogs despise.
