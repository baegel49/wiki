A shrinking realm, Brethil used to cover the central and northern portions of [[Osera]], before the conquering [[Thalen]] established the great realm of [[Thalendor]] in its southern half. Brethil is predominantly inhabited by the [[Brethiln]] people, who are, however, slowly being replaced by the Thalen and [[Altani]]. 

Brethil is subdivided into three smaller realms: [[Velwynne]], [[Mirena]], and [[Lonova]].
