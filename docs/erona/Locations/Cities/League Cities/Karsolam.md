Last-of-its-kind fortress city on the doomed continent of [[Hespenor]], Karsolam is both a refuge and a prison; its walls protect from the [[Horrors]] on the outside but keep its indentured population in place. Karsolam is a member of the [[Cenerian League]]. It is the only [[Mycean]] city with a majority population of [[Qedhi]]. 

#### Founding
[[Karsolam]] was originally built on the Khyx river and had a layout similar to many [[Mycean]] cities of its day. With the oncoming [[Collapse]], measures were taken to prepare the city, and its inner walls were arranged according to [[Geomancery]] principles by unknown builders. The city is now arranged in the form of a giant ward, resembling a glyph of warding, centred on the sacrificial plaza. There hangs suspended from chains the Blessed Martyr, a vessel for the magic which keeps the city's ward in place. Neither alive nor dead, the Blessed Martyr confers an uneven protection on the city. His announcements arrive with a shrieking pain, following which the ward often flickers and sometimes fails, until certain rites and rituals to [[Mhorr]] revitalise it. 

#### Population
Karsolam is relatively small, and is the only city of the [[Cenerian League]] with a majority [[Qedhi]] population. It is restricted by its walls, with its orchards and fields having to be tilled within them. Some underground excavation has taken place to harvest cave mushrooms and to build cisterns, which has allowed for a small amount of growth. 

#### Wards
Karsolam is unique for not having a [[Qedhi]] quarter as other [[Cenerian]] cities do. 

##### Central Plaza
The Central Plaza is the anchor for all of [[Karsolam]]. Suspended by six towers which ring it hangs the Blessed Martyr. The towers are connected via walkways to resemble two overlapping triangles, forming the central geometry of the ward of the city. Each of the walkways and towers have been carefully inscribed with epigraphs to strengthen the ward.
